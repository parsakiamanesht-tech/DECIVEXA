## Backlog Rules

1. Backlog items are not approved implementation scope.
2. No backlog item should bypass the Architecture Constitution.
3. Before promotion into active development, define value, boundary, dependencies, risks, evidence, and acceptance criteria.
4. During Development Freeze, backlog items remain deferred unless explicitly approved.
5. Ideas from conversations should be recorded here when they have long-term architectural value, preventing loss of strategic knowledge.
