2. Material architecture, scope, security, data-domain, AI, agent, or external-integration changes require renewed Founder approval and a decision record.

4. Real runtime verification is required for runtime-dependent claims.
5. Secrets must not be committed to Git.

7. AI providers remain behind the DECIVEXA AI Gateway.
8. Agent capabilities remain scoped, permissioned, auditable, cancellable, and policy-controlled.
9. No production deployment is authorized by this record.
